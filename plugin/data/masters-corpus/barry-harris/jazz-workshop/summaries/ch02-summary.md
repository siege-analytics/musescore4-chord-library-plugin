# Chapter 2 Summary

Chapter 2 teaches a foundational **Three-Step Solo Building** method: (1) choose and practice the correct scale(s) to outline the chord progression, which illuminates both harmony and improvisation pathways; (2) generate many phrases based on those scales, practicing them in all keys and tempos; (3) apply those phrases across different tunes using the same progression. Harris introduces three load-bearing harmonic definitions that anchor the exercises: the **Related Diminished Chord**, built a major 3rd above the dominant note, enables diminished-chord-extension techniques; **Important Minor**, the minor 7th chord on the 5th degree of a dominant 7th scale, provides a reusable harmonic tool for progressions like Rhythm Changes; and the **Minor 6 Diminished Scale**, constructed by combining a minor 6 chord with its related diminished chord, represents one of Harris's signature compositional-harmonic concepts. These three definitions form the harmonic vocabulary and calculation rules that students apply across the applications work that follows.
