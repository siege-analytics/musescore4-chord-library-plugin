# Chapter 4 — The Blues

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch04.md`._

Chapter 4 introduces the all-dominant turnaround rule, a structural principle where all chords in a turnaround sequence become dominant chords with varying voicings, each leading to the next until resolving to the tonic (I). This technique appears prominently in both Blues and Brazilian music traditions, representing a characteristic harmonic device that creates momentum through dominant chord chains. The chapter establishes the load-bearing terminology of 'all-dominant turnaround' and 'voicings' as essential concepts for understanding how these genres build harmonic tension and resolution within their chord progressions.
