## Key improvisation concept

> We can improvise over any chord or any sequence of chords belonging to any harmonic
> ;ield simply by using the Ionian or Aeolian Mode of the key we are in. We call this approach
> "key improvising" or "key improvisation". We will go deeper into the other modes later in
> our studies. For now, we will create the sounds of all the modes using the Ionian and
> Aeolian scales.

**Significance (p. 244).** Central thesis: Ionian + Aeolian cover all seven harmonic field modes.

## Five vertical zones — rationale

> One of the most dif-icult things for guitar player, is to master the scales and other melodic
> ideas all over the guitar fretboard. The reason for this is because the fingerings constantly
> change from one position to another. So in order to solve this problem, let’s split the fretboard
> into -ive vertical zones. It is important to understand that the vertical visualization is the
> key to playing horizontally, because it makes it possible to connect the fretboard zones
> going from one to another without getting lost. In other words, we visualize vertically but
> play horizontally most of the time.

**Significance (p. 246).** Signature five-zone system; visualize vertically, play horizontally.

## Guide chords for zone navigation

> Let’s start visualizing the Ionian and Aeolian scale (key & relative scale) in the five vertical
> zones. Each zone will be related with a chord shape (Cmaj7 or Am7). The chords will guide
> us better then just single notes, because they are -ixed structures, which are easier to
> remember rather than a lot of single notes. Make sure to use the root of each chord to
> guide you in each zone.

**Significance (p. 246).** Anchor scale zones to chord shapes for memory.

## Vertical vs. horizontal ideas

> Attention:
> Usually, the vertical ideas do not work as well as the horizontal ideas. However, we have some
> exceptions, and sometimes both work perfectly. Next we have many examples of Ionian and
> Aeolian scales vertically and horizontally. Choose the examples you like the most, memorize
> them and have fun.

**Significance (p. 246).** Explicit stylistic preference for horizontal over vertical.

> scales of the harmonic ;ields into these two scales.
