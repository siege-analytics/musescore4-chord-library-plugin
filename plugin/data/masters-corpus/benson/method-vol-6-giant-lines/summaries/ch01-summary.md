# Chapter 1 — The Secret of the Giant Lines

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch01.md`._

Chapter 1 introduces the foundational pedagogical principle that understanding chords is the gateway to harmonic mastery and improved single-note playing. Benson's characteristic approach—evident from the chapter's opening section "Chords leading to harmony"—is to build melodic fluency by first grounding the player in chord knowledge. The load-bearing concept introduced here is the chord-to-harmony-to-line progression: chords provide the harmonic foundation that enables deeper understanding of melody and single-line technique.
