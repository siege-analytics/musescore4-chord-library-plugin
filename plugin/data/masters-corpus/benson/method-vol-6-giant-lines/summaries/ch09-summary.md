# Chapter 9 — D7(#9) Giant Lines

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch09.md`._

Chapter 9 teaches how to create sophisticated outside effects over D7 chords using George Benson-style chord transitions, introducing techniques that employ substitution chords (A7, B7) and their associated scales to generate colorful harmonic tension. The master's characteristic approach visible here is the strategic use of dominant substitutions—specifically A7 ideas over D7 and B7 leading to Em—paired with precise durational control to maximize effect without destroying harmonic coherence; Benson's hallmark warning against staying on the substitution chord too long reflects his ear for the exact threshold where outside color tips into dissonance. Key load-bearing concepts introduced are the First and Second GB Chord Transition techniques, the D Mixolydian/B7 outside rule (grounded in the Emi chord family), and the critical Ending Note Rule requiring that all outside-effect lines resolve to a D7 chord tone. The chapter systematically balances theoretical justification (Mixolydian mode derivation from chord families) with practical compositional constraint, establishing both the harmonic vocabulary and the discipline needed to deploy it.
