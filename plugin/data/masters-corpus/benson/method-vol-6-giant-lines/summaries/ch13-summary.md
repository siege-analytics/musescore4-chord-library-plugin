# Chapter 13 — Dm7 Active Giant Lines

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch13.md`._

Chapter 13 teaches the Active Giant Lines concept applied to Dm7 harmony, focusing on how to construct melodic lines over dominant 7th chords within minor key progressions. The characteristic move visible here is the master's systematic treatment of relative major and minor key relationships—specifically, distinguishing which melodic options work over dominant chords like A7 (leading to Dm7) versus C7 (leading to Fmaj7), introducing the principle that major 7th superimposed ideas suit some contexts while creating harmonic tension in others. The chapter introduces the load-bearing terminology of "Active Giant Lines" (the melodic framework), "guide chord visualization," and the concept of dominant 7th chord options that "will work almout always"—establishing a decision-making filter for which lines are harmonically valid in a given cadential context. The underlying pedagogical move maps single-line fingerings onto guide chord shapes, grounding abstract melodic theory in practical fretboard application.
