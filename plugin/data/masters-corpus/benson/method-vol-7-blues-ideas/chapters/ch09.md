## Superimposed cadences losing V degree

> in general the superimposed IIm7, V7, I cadences lose the V degree and stay
> only with the IIm7 and the Imaj7. However, remember that we can always use the relative
> chords from minor and major chords.

**Significance (p. 53).** Central rule governing superimposition of cadences.

## Superimposition color options for IIm7/I cadence

> Let's look at cadence in the key of "C" (Dm7, G7, Cmaj7). Therefore we would have 4 different
> "colors" to use as superimposition ideas:
>
> • Dm7, Fmaj7 (relative of Dm7) Cmaj7 and Am7 (relative of Cmaj7).

**Significance (p. 53).** Concrete enumeration of superimposition colors using relative chords.
