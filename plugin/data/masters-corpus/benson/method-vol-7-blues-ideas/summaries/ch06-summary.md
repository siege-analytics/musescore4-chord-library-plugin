# Chapter 6 — Giant Lines – Going Deeper into the Flux & Reflux over D7(9)

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch06.md`._

Chapter 6 teaches three escalating layers of improvisation over D7(9): Blues Scale Ideas as the foundational technique, followed by Mixolydian, Lydian Dominant & Blues Ideas that integrate all three scale/mode sources simultaneously, and culminating in Superimposed Major 7th Chord Tricks that blend dominant 7th concepts (Blues Scale, Mixolydian, and Lydian Dominant) with maj7 arpeggios layered over dominant 7th chords. The master's approach demonstrates how to systematically deepen within a single harmonic context—the 'Flux & Reflux over D7(9)'—by progressively combining scalar and arpeggio vocabularies rather than introducing new chords. The load-bearing concepts are Blues Scale, Mixolydian mode, Lydian Dominant scale, superimposition of maj7 arpeggios, and the organizing principle of harmonic flux and reflux, which frames how tension and resolution unfold within the dominant 7th framework.
