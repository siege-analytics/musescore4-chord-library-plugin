# Chapter 12 — Dm7 Active Giant Lines

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch12.md`._

Chapter 12 (Dm7 Active Giant Lines) explores modal substitution techniques for dominant and diminished chords, where minor 7th cadences in Dorian mode replace functional harmonies while preserving altered tensions and introducing distinctive color notes. The chapter covers four characteristic substitution moves: Cm7/F7 for A half-diminished (justified through harmonic equivalence within Bb key, where Cm6 = F7(9) and Cm7 Dorian = Aø Locrian), C#m7/F#7 for C7 (leveraging altered tensions #9, b9, #11, b13 and the 'B' note as a major-7th color), F#m7 Dorian as a single-scale reading of the C#m7/F#7→C7 cadence (yielding a hybrid sound between C7 Dominant Diminished and C7alt), and Abm7/Db7 for C7 (highlighting dual color notes 'Cb (B)' and 'F' and introducing the consecutive minor-7th chord chain Gm7-Abm7-Am7). Key terminology includes Dorian scale applications, modal hybrid chords, altered tensions, color notes defined by dual interval function (e.g., a note functioning as both minor 3rd of one chord and major 6th of another), and minor-7th chord chains as a structural device. The chapter also emphasizes phrasing discipline: resting during turnarounds to separate blues choruses and allow breathing space between melodic 'stories.'
