## Common modulation targets

> Most standard tunes never modulate more than one accidental up or down. The keys most frequently modulated to from the key of C (no sharps or flats) are: A minor (no sharps or flats), F and D minor (one flat), G and E minor (one sharp). These other keys correspond to the diatonic triads of the key of C: Dm = ii, Em = iii, F = IV, G = V, Am =
> vi.
