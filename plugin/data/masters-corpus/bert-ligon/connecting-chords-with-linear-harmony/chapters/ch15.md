## Written component rationale

> Helps the students recognize the patterns in written form. Helps them prepare for improvising by
> learning the harmonic progression and identifying the tones necessary for modulation.

**Significance (p. 144).** Pedagogical rationale.

## Movable DOO articulation

> Have individuals scat solos through the tune based strictly on the outlines. Use the syllables DOO
> and DAHT (the jazz "movable DOO") to help them emphasize jazz articulations.

**Significance (p. 144).** Named technique for scat.

## Limiting as control

> Limiting is a way of learning control. Control is what all musicians and composers strive in any style
> or discipline.

**Significance (p. 144).** Philosophical justification.

## Outlines as foundation for exploration

> Outlines give them security in hearing and playing clear harmonic ideas in a linear fashion. Once
> they can hear and understand the basic path it will be easier to stray from that path and explore,
> knowing and hearing where to return.

**Significance (p. 144).** Pedagogical arc.

## Written variations build arsenal

> One or two new lines for each outline over a semester would give students quite an arsenal of
> musical ideas. Practicing them develops technique and dexterity.

**Significance (p. 144).** Quantifies semester benefit.
