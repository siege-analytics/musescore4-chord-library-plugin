# Chapter 10 — Outline Exercises

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch10.md`._

Chapter 10 teaches outline exercises as the foundation for jazz improvisation, emphasizing systematic practice with a metronome across all twelve major keys until achieving non-stop, error-free modulation through every key. The chapter establishes a generative practice methodology: internalize simple melodic outlines aurally by singing the changes away from the instrument, then apply embellishment devices to create more musical lines, practicing one outline at a time before combining multiple approaches. Key concepts include the aural-to-written-to-performed loop (writing down vocal improvisations to solidify them), recognizing turnaround progressions as universal structural elements, and identifying how jazz masters like Cannonball Adderley use devices to ornament underlying outlines. The master's characteristic move visible here is connecting theoretical outline practice directly to standard repertoire—learning three tunes (All the Things You Are, Cherokee, Body and Soul) forces complete 12-key mastery—bridging the gap between mechanical exercise and musicality through real jazz standards.
