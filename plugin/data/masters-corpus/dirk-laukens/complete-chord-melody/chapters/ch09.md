> Regardless of which fingers you use, ensure that both fingers sound the notes simultaneously.

**Significance (p. 298).** Core synchronization rule separating clean ensemble fingerstyle from ragged arpeggiation.

## String-skip fingering for 6ths

> Now you need to negotiate a string skip in the plucking hand, hence the use of pm and ia as marked.

## Wrist position for multi-string plucking

> Ensure that your wrist is rolled slightly more towards the headstock to allow more space between the
> index finger and thumb.

**Significance (p. 298).** Foundational hand-position rule preventing mechanical failure when plucking adjacent strings.

## Three-note chord fingering preference

> 3 notes at once, it’s preferable to use pi m, or i ma, so it’s worth practicing both these
> combinations.

**Significance (p. 298).** Specifies preferred finger groupings for three-voice chords.

## Little finger usage and four-finger maximum

> I rarely use the little finger (c) on the right hand with fingerstyle technique — I tend to stick to 4-note

**Significance (p. 298).** Establishes a practical voicing ceiling and justifies omitting the weak little finger.

## Mirror use for technique development

> Using a mirror to see what your hand is doing when you’re developing techniques like these is very

## Thumb double-pluck technique

> Notice that the thumb plays a note twice in a row. It’s quite natural for the thumb to do a double
> pluck in these situations as it simplifies the picking fingering greatly.

**Significance (p. 301).** Legitimizes and explains the thumb double-pluck as a deliberate simplifying strategy.

## Relaxed hand for three-string exercises

**Significance (p. 303).** Reinforces relaxation as the corrective principle when coordination breaks down.

## Thumb independence in picking patterns

> thumb independence in a picking pattern context —- the thumb moves

**Significance (p. 306).** Identifies thumb string-switching as a distinct skill targeted by the exercise.

## Bossa Nova etude practice sequence

> I would suggest practicing the picking pattern over a single chord for a while until you get it under

**Significance (p. 315).** Prescribes an isolation-then-integration practice sequence.

## Varying finger combinations on repeats
