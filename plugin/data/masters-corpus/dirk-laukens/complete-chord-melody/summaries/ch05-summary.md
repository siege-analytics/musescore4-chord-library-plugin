# Chapter 5 — MODULE 3 - LET'S CREATE A CHORD MELODY ARRANGEMENT!

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch05.md`._

Chapter 5 teaches the systematic method for creating solo guitar chord melody arrangements through a three-step melody-note identification procedure (string location, chord type, interval relationship), prioritizing playability over complexity as a musically superior choice. The master establishes that solo arranging succeeds through implication rather than completeness — alternating walking bass, chord lines, and single-note lines creates the illusion of a full band that the listener's mind completes. Core techniques include shell voicings (root, 3rd, 7th) as the harmonic foundation; strategic key selection to access open-string bass notes; sparse chord placement (less is more) at key moments; and refinement as the most critical stage, requiring small tweaks and skillful fingering to feel natural under the hands. The chapter introduces essential terminology and tools — arpeggiation for ballads, octaves with muting technique, diads as parallel-interval harmonization — while addressing the fundamental structural difference from trio arranging: the guitarist must supply the bass-note function themselves, making the 5th and 6th strings centrally important. Characteristic of this master is the philosophy that technical ease and musical excellence align; difficult voicings are solved by cutting notes rather than abandoning the chord, and the entire arrangement philosophy rests on what you omit rather than what you include.
