## Block chord effect: voicing must move with melody

> The block chord effect is best achieved when the chordal tones are made to move when the melody moves.
> and preferably in the same direction as the melody. Often the melody can be fingered easily without having
> to change the chord form, but this tends to destroy the block chord effect; therefore it is more desirable to
> change the voicing or inversion with each new melody note, in order to create the effect of all lines moving
> at once.

**Significance (p. 9).** Gives the rule for achieving block chord effect: change voicing/inversion with each melody note.
