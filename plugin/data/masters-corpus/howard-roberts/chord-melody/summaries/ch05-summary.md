# Chapter 5 — Chord Melody Solo

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch05.md`._

Chapter 5 is the closing solo of the method — a fully notated chord melody performance piece that demonstrates the cumulative application of every device introduced earlier (close and open voicings, parallel and contrary motion, counter lines, block chords, sustained backgrounds, short punctuations, octave jumps, chromatic substitutions). No new teaching prose appears; the chapter functions as a worked example, intended to be studied and absorbed as one would a transcription of a master performance. The pedagogical move is the inverse of the rest of the book: rather than stating rules and giving short examples, Roberts here lets the solo itself embody the integrated mature style toward which the entire method has been pointing.
