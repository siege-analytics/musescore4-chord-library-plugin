# Chapter 3 — KEY CENTERS

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch03.md`._

Chapter 3 teaches **key centers** — how stacking a major or minor scale in thirds creates diatonic harmony and how to identify which key a chord progression belongs to using analytical shortcuts like the dominant seventh chord. Students learn the **five movable scale patterns** that cover the entire fingerboard, organized by a **finger-per-fret** left-hand mechanic, with tonics circled for visual targeting and connection achieved through three core moves: position skips, stretching, and sliding on half steps. The chapter emphasizes developing a strong visual and auditory imprint of each pattern and shows how to unify arpeggio playing within the key-center framework rather than treating chords independently, with the ultimate goal of playing changes **by ear** rather than through intellectual calculation alone.
