# Chapter 6 — THE PROGRAM

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch06.md`._

Chapter 6 establishes the non-negotiable practices and mindset required for the method's success, centered on internalization, structured solo-building, and disciplined practice. Key techniques include **melody sketch over changes** to provide structural scaffolding, **question and answer form** as the foundational improvisation pattern, **sing before playing** to embed melodic ideas through ear-to-hand development, and diagnostics like **warm-up and string travel** work to prevent left/right-hand synchronization failures. The chapter addresses psychological obstacles: recognizing and breaking **repeated licks**, avoiding **blaming the wrong hand** when technique falters, and understanding the **growth plateau cycle** — the counterintuitive reality that subjective struggle signals neural activation while felt fluency signals rest periods. Critical enablers include **memorizing chord changes** to free cognitive resources for real-time musical decision-making and informal **noodling as learning** to validate low-pressure practice time. The chapter closes with an iron contract: **program adherence requirements** demand exact execution without deviation, and the **constant practice regimen** must remain unwavering through plateaus, making discipline and schedule consistency the ultimate load-bearing terms.
