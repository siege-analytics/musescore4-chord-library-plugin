# Chapter 7 — POINTERS

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch07.md`._

Chapter 7 establishes the foundational discipline and mindset required for the method's six-week speed-building project, covering the **eighth notes only rule** and **alternate picking requirement** as the technical constraints that isolate picking mechanics. Key pedagogical principles include **program adherence** (exact compliance is non-negotiable), the **21-day permanent imprinting threshold** (maintaining tempo for 21 days locks skills into the nervous system), and **blame attribution** (diagnosing problems on the correct hand before attempting fixes). The chapter addresses common practice obstacles: the **growth plateau cycle** (distinguishing felt progress from actual neurological development), **awkward tempos between feel zones** (recognizing that certain tempo ranges are structurally uncomfortable), and **environmental tuning interference** (auditing practice conditions before self-blame). Strategic guidance includes **metronome as tracker not target** (recording progress rather than chasing fixed speeds), **legato and steady time** (sustain and metronomic steadiness as non-negotiable execution standards), and **group practice as accelerator** (identifying ensemble work as superior to solo practice). The chapter concludes with **tempo objectives vs. accuracy** (subordinating speed goals to precision) and **key center analysis flexibility** (granting harmonic latitude and trusting ear-testing as the final arbiter of choice).
