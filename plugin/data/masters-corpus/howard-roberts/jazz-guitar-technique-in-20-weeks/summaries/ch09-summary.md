Chapter 9 (Week One Project Lesson 1-A) is overwhelmingly a notated etude/project-lesson chapter; the load-bearing didactic prose is brief but pivotal. It teaches that technique training serves **spontaneous invention** of solo lines rather than rigid adherence to examples.
