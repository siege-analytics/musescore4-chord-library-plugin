# Chapter 1 — CHORD FORMS

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch01.md`._

Chapter 1 contains no load-bearing prose passages — composed entirely of notated chord-diagram grids. The chapter (pages 6-14) presents Joe Pass's working voicing inventory across six taxonomic categories: Seventh Chord Forms, Augmented Chord Forms, Minor Chord Forms, Diminished Chord Forms, Minor Seventh Flat Fifth Chord Forms, and the implicit major/dominant alterations that lead each grid. Each voicing is rendered as a fretboard diagram with bottom-to-top pitch labels printed beneath; no text instruction accompanies them. The pedagogical move is presentation-by-catalog: Pass treats the chord shapes themselves as the lesson, expecting the student to absorb voicing vocabulary through silent inspection and finger-pattern memorization rather than through verbal explanation. This catalog functions as the raw vocabulary that Chapter 2 then deploys in chordal-movement passages.
