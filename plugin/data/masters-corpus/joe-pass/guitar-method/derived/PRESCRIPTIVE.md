# Prescriptive lessons — joe-pass × guitar-method

## dom7
The dom7 is the load-bearing harmonic category in Pass's system — every navigation, melodic, and voicing rule routes through it. Pass treats it as a family rather than a single chord: the Basic Chord Form with flatted seventh is the irreducible floor, and all extensions/alterations are color variants of the same dominant function, freely interchangeable by ear.

- [ch.7 p.22] dom7 is the explicit gateway to jazz phrasing, distinguished from major; primary lick vehicle.
- [ch.7 p.23] Basic Chord Form + flatted seventh is Pass's error-reduction floor for dominant improvisation.
- [ch.1 p.8] Against any dom7, freely incorporate b9, +9, b5, +5 (Altered Dominant 7th Scale); ear-verified.
- [ch.5 p.20] Three diminished scales pair exclusively with dom7 contexts.
- [ch.8 p.24] Joe's Blues palette: G13, G9, G9(6), G7+9, G7(b9) — freely substitutable for tonic G7.
- [ch.10 p.30] Three-or-four-note density ceiling; reject unnecessary thickness regardless of extensions.
- [ch.10 p.30] Voicings connect by stepwise voice motion or shared common tone.
- [ch.7 p.22] Dom7 licks transposed through ≥3 Chord Forms and all keys until singable.
- [ch.5 p.21] Melodic patterns must demonstrably grow out of the parent chord voicing.
- [ch.8 p.24] Tritone substitution: any member of E7 family interchangeable with any member of Bb9 family.
- [ch.8 p.24] Ear verification: check each improvised phrase against the chord believed operative.
- [ch.7 p.23] One Chord Form active in mind at all times when playing a dom7.

## dom7alt
Altered dominants are a spectrum of the dom7 family, fully interchangeable with simple dominants. In ii-V-I, alteration is structurally required — the chromatic voice motion it generates drives resolution.

- [ch.10 p.30] +9, b9, 13, 9(6), 11, ±5, b5 freely substitutable for simple dominants.
- [ch.11 p.31] G7(13)/G7(+5) generate chromatic voice motion required for resolution to Cmaj7; alteration load-bearing.
- [ch.1 p.8] Altered Dominant 7th Scale = simultaneous b9, +9, b5, +5; default altered-line vocabulary over any dom7.

## dom7b9
The b9 alteration makes the dominant functionally equivalent to a diminished chord, unlocking the Diminished Scale and enabling compound resolution. Pass pairs b9 and +9 together for improvisation.

- [ch.1 p.8] G7b9 is classified as diminished; b9 activates Diminished Scale eligibility; b9 and +9 travel together.
- [ch.8 p.24] G7(b9) one of five palette options substitutable for tonic G7 in Joe's Blues.
- [ch.11 p.31] G7(+5/b9) in Alison example B compounds b9 with +5 for maximum chromatic voice movement into C9(6).

## dom7#11
The #11 belongs to the same substitution family as all other altered dominant colors.

- [ch.10 p.30] 11th explicitly in Pass's substitution list as a valid replacement for a simple dominant.

## aug7 (dom7+5)
Augmented dominant is a structurally distinct variant with exclusive whole-tone scale pairing and a specific chromatic-intensifier role in ii-V-I.

- [ch.1 p.8] G7+5 augmented, linked exclusively to Augmented (whole-tone) Scale.
- [ch.5 p.20] Two whole-tone scales fit all dom7+5 chords; exhaustive and exclusive pairing.
- [ch.11 p.31] G7(+5) between G7(13) and Cmaj7 in ii-V-I template as chromatic intensifier.
- [ch.8 p.24] E+9 interchangeable with Bb9 tritone family; augmented quality does not exempt from tritone substitution.

## min7
Min7 functions in two roles: ii chord initiating ii-V-I voice-leading, and harmonically flexible pivot in blues turnarounds that can be recast as dominant.

- [ch.11 p.31] Dm7 canonical supertonic ii in Alison ii-V-I template.
- [ch.8 p.24] Bm7 in Joe's Blues bar 8 replaceable by F13/F9/F+9/Fm9 or recast to B7/B9/B13 — quality is a starting point, not a commitment.
- [ch.11 p.31 / ch.10 p.30] Dm7/Dm9 must connect into following dominant via stepwise motion or shared common tone.
- [ch.11 p.31] Bbm7 substitutes over underlying F7 backbone in Alison; min7 chosen for subdominant function voicing.

## min9
Extended form of supertonic ii in advanced voice-leading; ninth carried in ii chord for richer chromatic connection into altered dominant.

- [ch.11 p.31] Dm9 opens Dm9-C13-G7(+5/b9)-C9(6) chain in Alison.

## dim7
Dim7 is a functional alias for dom7b9 and a chromatic passing device — not an independent harmonic category.

- [ch.1 p.8] G7b9 classified as diminished; dim7 is the alteration-state of the dominant at b9.
- [ch.5 p.20] Three diminished scales pair exclusively with dominant/diminished contexts.
- [ch.12 p.32] In Grete, Edim is chromatic passing chord between Eb and Dm7 (Eb-Edim-Dm7-G7).

## maj7
Maj7 is the tonic resolution target throughout — reached by stepwise or common-tone voice motion from altered dominant. No special improvisational doctrine; voicing/density/connection rules identical to every other quality.

- [ch.11 p.31] Cmaj7 tonic endpoint of canonical Dm7-G7(13)-G7(+5)-Cmaj7 ii-V-I.
- [ch.10 p.30 / ch.11 p.31] Three-or-four-note density ceiling; Bbmaj7/Ebmaj7 conform.
- [ch.11 p.31] Ebmaj7 substitutes over backbone Eb chord in Alison (F13-Bbm7-Ebmaj7 over C7-F7-Bbmaj7-Eb).
- [ch.11 p.31] In Alison example B, resolution target extended to C9(6); tonic absorbs chromatic voice motion via ninth/sixth.
- [ch.11 p.31] Triplet rhythmic framework governs all voice-leading moves into maj7 in Alison.
