# Chapter 1 — VOLUME ONE: NAME THAT CHORD

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch01.md`._

Chapter One presents a comprehensive reference catalog rather than instructional prose, systematically organizing voicings for triads, seventh chords, and two-note bases across six cycles (2-7) within three parent scales: C Major, C Melodic Minor, and C Harmonic Minor. Each voicing is displayed in both close and spread positions, with accompanying analytical data including intervallic voice-leading relationships, functional voice-leading information, and Most Stepwise Resolution Pattern (M.S.R.P.) strings. Spanning pages 12-189, this chapter functions as a lookup resource designed to exhaustively catalog chord voicing possibilities and their systematic transformations across different harmonic contexts. Rather than teaching voice-leading principles through prose explanation, Goodrick establishes a structured, table-based reference that guitarists can consult to understand voicing relationships, resolution patterns, and the intervallic consequences of different positional choices.
