# Chapter 3 — WELCOME TO VOLUME II

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch03.md`._

This introductory chapter establishes Volume II's methodological foundation by explaining why chords are left unnamed — to avoid limiting their harmonic function — and introduces the exhaustive taxonomy of 4-part chord families (7th, TBN I, TBN II, 4-part 4ths, and spread clusters) as the complete set of possibilities within the three diatonic scales (Major, Melodic Minor, Harmonic Minor). The chapter's defining characteristic is its shift from Volume I's triad-based architecture to structures built on 4th and 2nd intervals, which create greater harmonic ambiguity and functional flexibility. A critical usage rule invites readers to apply Volume II's concepts retroactively to Volume I, demonstrating that the framework is holistic rather than sequential. The content itself centers on voice-leading these five chord families through all six cycles in each of the three scale systems, establishing both the structural completeness and practical scope of the entire method.
