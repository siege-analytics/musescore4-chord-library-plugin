# Chapter 4 — WHAT IS THE M-LODE?

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch04.md`._

Chapter 4 introduces the M-Lode (Mother Lode), the complete system encompassing all material from both volumes: triad-based structures from Volume I (triads, 7th chords, TBN I, TBN II) combined with the 3-part and 4-part structures from Volume II (3-part 4ths, 4-part 4ths, spread clusters), all voice-led through six cycles in the three diatonic scales of C Major, C Melodic Minor, and C Harmonic Minor. The master's characteristic move here is establishing structural closure: he proves that these five 4-part chord families are the only 4-part families possible within the three diatonic 7-note scales, grounding the entire system in theoretical exhaustiveness. The unifying principle is that all triad-based structures in Volume I are triads plus additional notes, while 4-part structures become the primary focus with 3-part structures positioned as supplements. This chapter establishes both the nomenclature (M-Lode) and the theoretical foundation that validates the systematic scope of the entire method.
