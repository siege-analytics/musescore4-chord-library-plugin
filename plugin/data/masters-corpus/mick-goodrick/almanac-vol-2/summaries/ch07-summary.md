# Chapter 7 — THINGS TO DO WITH THE M-LODE

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch07.md`._

Chapter 7 teaches systematic combinatorial practice with the M-Lode by having students play all 6 possible combinations of 2 voices and then all 4 possible combinations of 3 voices, building methodical command of the four-voice structure. The chapter introduces a load-bearing practice method — exhaustive voice-combination exercises forcing internalization of how voices interact and move within the M-Lode framework. This combinatorial approach turns voicing practice into a structured completeness-driven curriculum. The chapter explicitly scopes where M-Lode applies: everywhere in both books except 18 pages of triads and 30 pages of 3-part 4ths.
