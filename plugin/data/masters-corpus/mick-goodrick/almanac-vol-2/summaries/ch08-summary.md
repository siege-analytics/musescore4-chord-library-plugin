# Chapter 8 — PICK VS. FINGERS

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch08.md`._

Chapter 8 contrasts finger-style and pick-based guitar playing, establishing that finger-style guitarists have significant technical advantages over pick-only players. The chapter teaches the practical capabilities of finger-style: partial and full barres (1/3, 1/2, full), and selectively plucking only the strings needed for a given voicing. The chapter introduces the key concept of octave insertion — placing an octave between any two voices within a 3-part or 4-part chord to expand harmonic options. This principle extends beyond simple triads to 4th voicings, making it load-bearing across multiple chord types.
