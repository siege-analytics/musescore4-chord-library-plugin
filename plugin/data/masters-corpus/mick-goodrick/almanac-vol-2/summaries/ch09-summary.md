# Chapter 9 — OCTAVE INSERTION

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch09.md`._

Chapter 9 introduces octave insertion, a voicing technique where a guitarist inserts an octave between any two voices within a 3-part or 4-part chord, significantly expanding textural possibilities for finger-style players. The chapter establishes that finger-style guitarists have a fundamental advantage over pick-only players because they can access unconventional voicings — counterpoint, spread triads, and unusual chord distributions. Guitarists already employ these voicings intuitively as part of 4-part chords; this chapter formalizes octave insertion as an explicit principle applicable to both triadic and 3-part 4th voicings. The technique bridges existing playing knowledge with new conceptual language, grounding the rule in the physical capabilities of finger-style technique.
