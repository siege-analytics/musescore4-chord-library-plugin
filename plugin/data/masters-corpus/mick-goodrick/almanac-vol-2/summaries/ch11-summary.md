# Chapter 11 — A USEFUL TIP

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch11.md`._

Chapter 11 introduces additional voicing options and a practical study tool. It explores Drop 4 (varying difficulty), the Double Drop 2/Drop 4 hybrid technique (octave inserted between the middle two voices of a Drop 2), and Drop 3 and 4 as advanced finger-style-only. Load-bearing terminology includes the octave-insertion technique and the named hybrid voicings. To support practice, the chapter details the index card method — eight 1/2-inch strips cut from a 4x6 card used to selectively cover lines and work through 2-part and 3-part voicing options. This reflects the master's characteristic move of pairing theoretical concepts with tangible, low-tech study aids.
