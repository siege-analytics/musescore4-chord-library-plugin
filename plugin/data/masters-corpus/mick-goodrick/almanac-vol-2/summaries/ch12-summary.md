# Chapter 12 — JOE'S 2 PLUS 2 THING

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch12.md`._

Chapter 12 introduces Joe's 2 Plus 2 Thing, a technique for breaking down 4-part chords into call-and-response musical phrases. The method divides a 4-part chord into two alternating 2-note groupings that function as question-answer pairs, creating a conversational interplay within harmonic movement. This exemplifies treating chord construction as a dialogue between musical phrases rather than a static vertical structure. The technique introduces the vocabulary of 2-note groupings and the call-and-response pattern as an organizational principle for voicing.
