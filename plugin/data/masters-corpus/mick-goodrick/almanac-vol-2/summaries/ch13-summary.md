# Chapter 13 — A SMALL IDEA WITH LARGE IMPLICATIONS

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch13.md`._

This chapter introduces the passing tone rule as a foundational principle: when any voice moves by a third, fill that interval with a passing tone — a technique that scales from solo melodies through counterpoint to full 4-part voicings. The master demonstrates this rule's power through multiple applications, establishing it as a single generative principle with wide applicability. A notable extension occurs when two voices move simultaneously in thirds, a rare but valid situation. The chapter anchors the technique in practical ensemble playing (VERSION A as a guitar duo) and introduces method-specific terminology — Joe's 2 Plus 2 Thing (VERSION B). This is the kind of 'small idea with large implications' the chapter title promises.
