# Chapter 15 — MORE ABOUT ARPEGGIOS

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch15.md`._

This chapter names three additional 3-part chord families within the M-Lode beyond triads and 3-part 4ths: 3-part Spread Clusters, 7th Chord (no 5th), and 7th Chord (no 3rd). The derivation method is concrete — drop one voice from any 4-part chord, with 4 possible configurations. The chapter argues these voicings are among the most guitar-friendly, retaining the method's characteristic voice-leading sound while being easier to play. It also gives prescriptive arpeggio rules: start with 4-way close options, prefer C Harmonic Minor (sounds better), exploit the 24 possible arpeggio sequences per 4-part voicing, and use up/down alternation across successive chords.
