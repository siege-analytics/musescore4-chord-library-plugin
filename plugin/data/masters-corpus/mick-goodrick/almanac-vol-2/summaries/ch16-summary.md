# Chapter 16 — HOUSTON, WE HAVE A PROBLEM

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch16.md`._

This chapter addresses voice-leading caveats and quantifies the M-Lode's scope. 4-way close is difficult on guitar; the rule is to find as many open-string playable voicings as possible. Cycles 2 and 7 carry two different voice-leadings — neither sonically pleasing — because of the diatonic 5th interval that risks voice-crossings between inversions. Spread cluster voicings are interesting and mostly unknown but physically demanding (BE VERY CAREFUL). The chapter states the foundational principle: 'All of the M-Lode is based on voice-leading' and claims all 3-part and 4-part voicings in Major, Melodic Minor, and Harmonic Minor have been found, with statistics: 20 different 3-part chords (240 voicings), 30+ different 4-part chords (800+ voicings), 1000+ total voicings.
