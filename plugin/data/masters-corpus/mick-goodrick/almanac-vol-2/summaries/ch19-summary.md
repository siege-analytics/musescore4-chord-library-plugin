# Chapter 19 — 3-PART 4THS: Close & Spread 6 Cycles C Major

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch19.md`._

This is the first large notation chapter: 3-part 4ths, Close & Spread voicings across all 6 Cycles in C Major. The chapter teaches via paired Intervallic/Functional Voice-Leading rule grids attached to each cycle/voicing combination. For each voicing the student gets explicit per-voice motion (e.g. '5 → 2nd, 2 → 2nd, 1 → 2nd' intervallic; '5 → 1, 2 → 5, 1 → 2' functional) plus an M.S.R.P. (memorized note-sequence) drill loop. Close to Spread alternates introduce the c.t. (common tone) notation. The chapter then pivots to 4-part 4ths with 4-way close and Drop 2 voicings, establishing the full per-voice grid template repeated throughout subsequent chapters.
