# Chapter 23 — 4-PART 4THS: 6 Voicing Types 6 Cycles C Melodic Minor

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch23.md`._

This chapter covers 4-part 4ths across all 6 cycles in C Melodic Minor. The book's dual-analysis system (intervallic + functional voice-leading) is demonstrated at scale. Spread Clusters Drop 2 over Cycle 2 shows that the 7th voice moves by a 4th while all other voices hold as common tones. Cycle 7 Drop 2 isolates the single non-common-tone voice (the 3rd moving by a 4th, resolving functionally to 7). Cycle 5 Drop 2 demonstrates the opposite case — all four voices move with alternating 3rd and 2nd motion, no common tones — making it a high-motion contrast to Cycle 7.
