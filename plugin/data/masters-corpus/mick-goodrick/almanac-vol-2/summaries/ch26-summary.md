# Chapter 26 — SPREAD CLUSTERS: 6 Voicing Types 6 Cycles C Melodic Minor

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch26.md`._

This chapter covers Spread Clusters across all 6 cycles in C Melodic Minor. The foundational dual intervallic/functional voice-leading framework governs every voicing entry: Cycle 4 4-way close (7 → 3rd, 3 → 2nd, 2 → 2nd, 1 → 3rd) elaborates downstream into Drop 2, Drop 3, and Drop 2 & 4 variants. Cycle 7 Drop 2 introduces a common-tone-dominant voicing where three voices sustain while only the 3rd moves by a fourth. Cycle 6 4-way close functional voice-leading (7 → 1, 3 → 7, 2 → 3, 1 → 2) shows each chord tone resolving by step to the next adjacent scale degree.
