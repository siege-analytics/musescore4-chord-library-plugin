# Chapter 2 — Name That Chord: Some Names for the Nameless Chords in Volume II

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch02.md`._

Chapter 2 introduces the foundational terminology system for analyzing chord structures and melodic patterns, defining key concepts like intervallic, functional, and diatonic voice-leading alongside distinctions between events (2–6 diatonic notes) and episodes (all 7 diatonic notes). The chapter establishes that events and episodes behave distinctly under diatonic transposition—events may become intervallically identical while episodes correspond to different modes—and clarifies that length alone does not determine their classification. A central practical contribution is the author's naming convention for stacked-fourth chords, advocating for sus4 notation (e.g., G [sus4] or B diminished [sus4]) as clearer and more musician-friendly than complex alterations or contemporary shorthand like C2. This terminological framework serves as the vocabulary backbone for all subsequent analytical work in the volume, grounding the method's approach to chord naming and voice-leading analysis in accessibility and intervallic precision.
