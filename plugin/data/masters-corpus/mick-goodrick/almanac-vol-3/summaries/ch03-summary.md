# Chapter 3 — The Real, Compleat M-Lode

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch03.md`._

These are some of the most interesting and difficult voicings within the M-Lode system, and Chapter 3 establishes the complete taxonomy by introducing three new 3-part chord categories: 7th chords (no 3rd), 7th chords (no 5th), and 3-part spread clusters. The chapter addresses the naming complexity problem that arises from multiple valid chord symbol interpretations of a single note set by proposing a practical shorthand: simply name clusters by their literal pitch names (e.g., "C D E") rather than forcing them into traditional chord nomenclature. This pragmatic approach reflects the author's characteristic move of prioritizing functional utility over theoretical purity. The complete M-Lode framework now encompasses triads and 7th chords alongside 3-part 4ths and spread clusters, with their 4-part structural counterparts, creating a comprehensive system for understanding and voicing chords on the guitar.
