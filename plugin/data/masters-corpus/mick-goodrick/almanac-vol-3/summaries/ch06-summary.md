# Chapter 6 — 3-Part Chords Derived From a TBN I

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch06.md`._

This chapter introduces the foundational concept of deriving 3-part chords from a Triadic Basis Note (TBN I), establishing a core harmonic principle for chord construction on the guitar. Though the chapter is primarily a notation/voicing-grid reference rather than prose, it teaches the systematic method of building three-note voicings using Drop 2, Drop 3, Drop 2&3, Drop 2&4, and Double Drop 2 Drop 3 derivations across root and inverted positions. The TBN framework provides students with a structural approach to chord formation that emphasizes the relationship between the foundational note and the intervals that define basic harmonic quality. The terminology introduced here becomes essential for understanding how larger harmonic structures decompose into playable, musical fragments.
