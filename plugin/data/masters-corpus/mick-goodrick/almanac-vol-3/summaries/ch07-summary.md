# Chapter 7 — 3-Part Chords Derived From a TBN II

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch07.md`._

3-Part chords derived from TBN II form the core instructional content of this chapter, building on the foundational triad construction methods introduced earlier by showing how to extract and voice three-note harmonies from a parent structure. The chapter is primarily a notation/voicing-grid reference, presenting Drop 2, Drop 3, Drop 2&3, Drop 2&4, and Double Drop 2/Drop 3 derivations of the G triad across inversions. This method represents a characteristic pedagogical move in the text: distilling complex harmonic material into rule-based procedures that students can replicate across the fretboard. By mastering this derivation technique, players gain both a practical vocabulary of voicings and a deeper grasp of harmonic logic.
