# Goodrick, Mick — Almanac of Guitar Voice Leading, Vol. 4 — Statement of Outputs

## Overview

*Almanac of Guitar Voice Leading, Volume 4* constructs a unified theoretical vocabulary for guitar before introducing a single chord or exercise. The book's architecture is deliberately accumulative: Chapters 1–3 establish the full analytical lexicon (IVL, FVL, GDVL, SCVL, M.S.R.P., Event, Episode), Chapters 4–9 deploy that lexicon across a systematic 840-chord catalog organized by the cycle-3/cycle-4 alternation principle, and Chapters 10–11 pivot to chromatic voice-leading and close with the method's governing philosophical claim — that excellent voice-leading treats melody and harmony as equals.

The method's characteristic theoretical move is generative rather than enumerative. The student who masters Event and Episode transposition rules can derive an entire modal landscape from a single starting point; the student who masters the reductive voice-omission principle can reconstruct the chord taxonomy from first principles.

Three interlocking systems emerge from the distillation: a **Voice-Leading Taxonomy System** that classifies all voice-leading types and enforces the melody-harmony parity principle; an **Event / Episode Modal Transposition System** that governs generative diatonic and modal derivation; and a **Chord Construction and Catalog System** that governs chord density, derivation, and catalog organization.
