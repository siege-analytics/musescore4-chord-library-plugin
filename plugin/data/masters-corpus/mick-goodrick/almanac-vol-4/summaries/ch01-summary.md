# Chapter 1 — TERMS

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch01.md`._

Chapter 1 introduces the foundational terminology and taxonomies that structure the guitar method's voice-leading framework. The chapter defines key abbreviations — IVL (intervallic voice-leading), FVL (functional voice-leading), GDVL (generic diatonic voice-leading), SCVL (specific chromatic voice-leading), and M.S.R.P. (melodic strand replication procedure) — establishing the analytical categories that organize the system's approach to melody, counterpoint, and harmony. Critical load-bearing concepts include the transposition properties of Events and Episodes: Events can be diatonically transposed to create six others, though some are intervallically identical, while Episodes can be transposed to create six others with one-to-one correspondence to the seven modes. The chapter clarifies that both Events and Episodes operate across melodic, contrapuntal, and harmonic domains interchangeably, and dispels the misconception that length defines the distinction between them. Grip-slipping is also introduced as a named technique for later elaboration.
