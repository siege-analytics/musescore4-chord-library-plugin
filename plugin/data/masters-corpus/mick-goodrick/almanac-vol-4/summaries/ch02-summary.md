# Chapter 2 — EVENT/EPISODE

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch02.md`._

Chapter 2 introduces event and episode as foundational terms for analyzing guitar music: events can be diatonically transposed to yield 6 variants (though some collapse into intervallic unisons), while episodes can be transposed to yield 6 variants that each map one-to-one to the seven modes. Both structures operate across melodic, contrapuntal, and harmonic domains. The master's characteristic move is the principle of modal correspondence — each transposed episode unlocks a different modal color. Load-bearing terminology includes diatonic transposition, intervallically identical, modes, and the author's preferred sus4 chord nomenclature (extended to all inversions), advocated for musician legibility.
