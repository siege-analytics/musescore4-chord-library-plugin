# Chapter 3 — NAME THAT CHORD: SOME NAMES FOR THE NAMELESS CHORDS IN VOLUME II

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch03.md`._

Chapter 3 establishes a naming convention for previously unnamed chords in Volume II by advocating for sus4 labeling over shorter notations like C2 or add-2, prioritizing clarity and wider musician understanding. The author extends sus4 naming across both 3-part and 4-part 4th structures with straightforward application. The chapter's central achievement is introducing three new 3-part chord types — 7th (no 3rd), 7th (no 5th), and 3-part Spread Clusters — which expand Volume II's foundational M-Lode into the complete chord taxonomy that underpins Volume III's methods.
