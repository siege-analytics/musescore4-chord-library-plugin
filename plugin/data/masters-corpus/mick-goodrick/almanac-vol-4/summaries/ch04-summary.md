# Chapter 4 — 3-PART CHORDS: FOUND AND IDENTIFIED!

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch04.md`._

Chapter 4 teaches the generative principle of deriving 3-part chords by removing one voice from a 4-part chord — a foundational method for understanding chord construction and reduction. The master's characteristic move is systematic elimination: by omitting each voice in turn, the student discovers that each 3-part chord type appears exactly four times, revealing the symmetric structure underlying the relationship between 4-part and 3-part voicing. Key terminology includes 4-part chord, 3-part chord, and voice removal. This reductive approach (omitting one voice) becomes the load-bearing principle that organizes the entire chapter and illuminates how harmonic density relates to chord identity.
