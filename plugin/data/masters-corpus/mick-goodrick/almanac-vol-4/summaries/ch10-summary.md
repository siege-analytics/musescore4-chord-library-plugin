# Chapter 10 — CHORD PROGRESSIONS AND APPLICATIONS

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch10.md`._

Chapter 10 introduces chord progressions and their applications while establishing a fundamental theoretical distinction in voice-leading approaches. The chapter teaches the difference between SCVL (specific chromatic voice-leading), which governs the movement between chords through non-diatonic voice paths, and GDVL (generic diatonic voice-leading), the foundational method covered in Volumes 1 and 2. The key pedagogical move is positioning IVLs and FVLs as specialized instances of SCVL, marking the transition from purely diatonic harmony to chromatic voice-leading techniques. This distinction serves as the load-bearing framework for understanding how chord progressions function beyond the diatonic palette.
