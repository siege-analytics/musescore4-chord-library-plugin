# Chapter 11 — REFERENCE AND INDEX

_Distillation; backed by `chapters/ch11.md`._

Chapter 11 serves as a reference and index to the entire method, and presents the author's foundational philosophical stance that distinguishes this approach from traditional guitar pedagogy. The chapter's key assertion is that excellent voice-leading treats melody and harmony as equals, rather than privileging melody over harmony as conventional theory does. This position on voice-leading and the equilibrium between melodic and harmonic elements represents the author's master principle, challenging the traditional hierarchy and establishing the thesis that underpins the method's distinctive approach to guitar instruction.
